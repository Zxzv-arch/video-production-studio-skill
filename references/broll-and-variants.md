# B-roll and derivative versions

Use for semantic B-roll, generated visuals, highlight clips, trailers, Shorts, Reels, and multi-platform delivery.

## B-roll purpose test

Every insert must prove a claim, demonstrate a process, establish context, visualize a relationship, hide an editorial cut, or provide emotional and pacing contrast. Avoid literal stock footage that merely repeats the noun being spoken.

## Source ladder

Prefer sources in this order:

1. User-provided footage, screenshots, documents, and product captures.
2. Locally generated diagrams, charts, UI mockups, code views, maps, and typography.
3. Properly licensed stock with attribution tracked when required.
4. Generated images animated with camera moves, layers, or parallax.
5. Generated video when the user authorizes the provider, cost, and upload/privacy implications.

Do not imply that synthetic footage documents a real event. Avoid unlicensed logos, music, clips, or scraped media.

## Shot specification

For each B-roll beat, record purpose, source, in/out time, framing, motion, duration, and transition. Target enough visual change to support attention without replacing every sentence with a new shot.

## Highlight extraction

Score candidates by standalone clarity, emotional or informational payoff, hook strength, context required, visual support available, and clean boundaries. Do not select a provocative fragment that reverses the speaker's meaning.

## Platform variants

Derive variants from an approved master, but redesign composition for each aspect ratio:

- **16:9:** room for diagrams, split layouts, and longer captions.
- **9:16:** one hero region, larger text, face-safe center framing, shorter caption chunks.
- **1:1:** compact hierarchy and fewer simultaneous side panels.

Re-check every caption, crop, and graphic after reframing. Automatic center-crop is only a draft. Keep variant IDs and differences in the project manifest so exports are reproducible.
